Privacy policy

Effective November 12, 2026. This policy explains what information BusinessForYou.com collects, why, who we share it with and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

BusinessForYou.com ("we", "us") is an independent information and introduction service for people buying, selling and financing small businesses. You can reach us about privacy through our contact form; choose "Something else" and start your message with "Privacy".

2. Information you give us

When you use a form, such as buyer matching, a valuation request, financing pre-qualification, a listing submission, a contest entry, a job application, a support pledge, the newsletter or the contact form, we collect what you enter. Depending on the form this can include your name, email address, phone number, location, budget ranges, business details and your message. Please do not submit bank, card or government identification numbers in any form.

3. Information collected automatically

  • Advertising and analytics cookies. Google and its partners may set cookies or use similar technologies to serve and measure ads (see section 6).
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Browser storage. Our calculators, due diligence checklist and cookie choice are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. We cannot see this data, and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

4. How we use information

  • To answer your request and send matches, value ranges or financing options.
  • To make introductions to brokers, lenders, buyers or franchise companies, only after you approve each one.
  • To send the newsletter you subscribed to (unsubscribe at any time).
  • To run contests, review job applications and process support pledges.
  • To keep the site secure, prevent spam and fraud, and comply with law.
  • To understand which content is useful and improve the site.

5. Who we share information with

  • Form processing. Form submissions are relayed to our inbox by a form-delivery service acting on our behalf.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages.
  • Google. For advertising (AdSense) and web fonts.
  • YouTube. Videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced player and load only when you press play. YouTube's own policies then apply.
  • Partners you approve. A specific broker, lender, buyer or franchise company, only after you agree to that introduction.
  • Legal reasons. When required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

When you choose "Essential only" in our cookie notice, we ask Google to show non-personalised ads. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland may also see a consent message managed through Google's certified consent tools.

7. How long we keep information

We keep form submissions for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless an ongoing relationship or the law requires longer. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and UK GDPR, California privacy law, Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec's privacy law, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. To make a request, use the contact form. We will respond within the time the law requires and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

9. Security and transfers

The site is served over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please share only what is needed. Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, with safeguards appropriate to the law.

10. Children

The site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed; material changes will be highlighted on this page.
