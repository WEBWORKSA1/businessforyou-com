A business that is right for you, not just for sale

BusinessForYou.com exists for the person on the other side of the table from the professionals: the first-time buyer, the owner selling once in a lifetime. We give them the same numbers, checklists and introductions the professionals use, free.

What we do

  • Tools that mirror how brokers and lenders evaluate deals: valuation, loan coverage, payback and due diligence.
  • Guides in plain English for every stage of buying, financing and selling.
  • A listings board where owners can list for free and buyers can see the numbers that matter.
  • Opt-in introductions to brokers, lenders and franchise opportunities, made only when you ask.
  • Community: monthly contests, a video library and a weekly newsletter.

How we are funded

We believe you should know how a free site pays its bills before you trust it:

  • Advertising. Google ads appear on most pages. Ads are selected by Google and do not influence our content or recommendations.
  • Partner fees. Some brokers, lenders and franchise companies pay a referral fee when an introduction becomes a client. We disclose that relationship when we make the introduction, and it never costs you more.
  • Sponsorships. Firms can sponsor categories, contests and the newsletter. Sponsored placements are labelled.
  • Supporters. Readers who pledge support help fund hosting, promotion, talent and prizes.

Editorial standards

  • We cite the source of market data, such as the BizBuySell Insight Report or the U.S. Small Business Administration, and link to it.
  • Guides are original work. We do not copy third-party articles, and we embed videos only through YouTube's official player.
  • Sponsors and partners do not see or approve content before publication.
  • We correct errors promptly. If you spot one, tell us.
  • Example listings are always labelled as examples.

What we are not

BusinessForYou.com is not a business broker, lender, investment adviser, franchise seller, law firm or accounting firm. We do not hold deposits or negotiate on anyone's behalf. Read the full disclaimer.
