20 businesses to buy, and what to check first

Every business type has its own way of making money and its own way of hiding problems. Use this field guide to narrow your search, then verify the specific risks before you make an offer.

Laundromat

Self-service and wash-and-fold revenue from machines in a leased unit. Popular with first-time buyers who want limited staff.

Profit drivers
Machine age, utility costs, pricing, wash-and-fold and commercial accounts
Verify
Water and gas bills against claimed revenue, machine counters, lease length
Watch for
Ageing machines that need replacing soon after you buy

HVAC and plumbing services

Repair, replacement and maintenance work for homes and businesses. Strong demand and recurring service agreements.

Profit drivers
Maintenance agreements, technician productivity, average ticket
Verify
Licences held by the owner versus staff, agreement renewals, warranty claims
Watch for
The licence leaving with the seller; technician turnover

Commercial cleaning

Contracts to clean offices, clinics and buildings, usually at night with hourly crews.

Profit drivers
Contract length, route density, labour cost and supervision
Verify
Contracts and their notice periods, customer concentration, payroll compliance
Watch for
Contracts that can be cancelled with 30 days' notice after the sale

Landscaping and lawn care

Recurring residential and commercial maintenance plus higher-margin design and installation work.

Profit drivers
Recurring maintenance share, crew efficiency, equipment condition
Verify
Seasonality by month, equipment list, customer retention
Watch for
Revenue tied to one large commercial client

Car wash

Express tunnel, in-bay automatic or self-serve washes, often with monthly membership plans.

Profit drivers
Membership count, traffic counts, chemical and water costs
Verify
Membership churn, equipment age, whether real estate is included, zoning
Watch for
A new competitor approved nearby

Auto repair shop

Maintenance and repair for local drivers and fleets. Reputation and technician skill drive everything.

Profit drivers
Labour rate, bay utilisation, parts margin, fleet accounts
Verify
Technician certifications, environmental compliance, online reviews
Watch for
Contamination or disposal issues at the site

Childcare centre

Licensed care for infants to school-age children. Waitlists are common, regulation is heavy.

Profit drivers
Enrolment, staff-to-child ratios, tuition rates, subsidies
Verify
Licence transfer process, inspection reports, staff qualifications
Watch for
Director or key educators leaving with the seller

Healthcare practice

Dental, physiotherapy, optometry or veterinary clinics. Often must be owned or run by licensed professionals.

Profit drivers
Patient base, payer mix, clinician productivity
Verify
Ownership rules in your jurisdiction, patient retention, billing compliance
Watch for
Revenue that depends on the selling clinician staying

Insurance agency

A book of policies that pays renewal commissions every year. Valued heavily on retention.

Profit drivers
Renewal rate, commission splits, carrier appointments
Verify
Carrier contracts and transferability, retention by line, licensing
Watch for
Carriers that will not appoint a new owner

Accounting or bookkeeping practice

Recurring monthly bookkeeping, payroll and tax work for small businesses and households.

Profit drivers
Recurring fees, staff utilisation, software efficiency
Verify
Client retention after past ownership changes, seasonality, credentials needed
Watch for
Clients loyal to the seller personally rather than the firm

Marketing or digital agency

Retainer and project work for clients. Low assets, high dependence on people.

Profit drivers
Retainer share, client tenure, delivery margins
Verify
Client contracts, concentration, who manages each account
Watch for
Founder-led sales with no pipeline after the founder leaves

Ecommerce brand

Physical products sold online through its own store and marketplaces.

Profit drivers
Gross margin, repeat customers, ad efficiency, supplier terms
Verify
Live store and payment data, inventory, supplier agreements, account health
Watch for
Sales that collapse when ad spend is reduced

Content or affiliate website

Earns from display ads and affiliate commissions on search and social traffic.

Profit drivers
Organic traffic, revenue per thousand visits, content quality
Verify
Analytics and earnings dashboards live, traffic sources, backlink profile
Watch for
Traffic dependence on one search update or platform

Micro-SaaS

Small software products with subscription revenue and low running costs.

Profit drivers
Monthly recurring revenue, churn, support load
Verify
Billing data, code quality and documentation, third-party dependencies
Watch for
A product built on a platform that can change its rules

Coffee shop or cafe

High-traffic, low-ticket food and drink. Margins depend on location and labour.

Profit drivers
Foot traffic, average ticket, labour scheduling, wholesale accounts
Verify
Point-of-sale data, lease, health inspections, equipment
Watch for
Owner working unpaid hours that a buyer would have to pay for

Restaurant

Full- or limited-service dining. Demanding to run but often priced attractively.

Profit drivers
Food and labour cost percentages, covers, rent
Verify
Sales records against bank deposits, liquor licence transfer, lease
Watch for
Unreported cash sales that cannot be verified or financed

Gym or fitness studio

Membership revenue plus training and classes. Independent or franchised.

Profit drivers
Active members, churn, coach retention, rent
Verify
Billing system data, prepaid memberships owed, equipment leases
Watch for
Prepaid memberships you must honour without receiving the cash

Vending and ATM routes

Machines placed in host locations and serviced on a route. Low staff, travel-heavy.

Profit drivers
Placement agreements, machine revenue, route density
Verify
Location agreements, per-machine sales history, machine condition
Watch for
Placements that can be withdrawn at will

Self-storage

Rental income from storage units. Part business, part real estate.

Profit drivers
Occupancy, rent per unit, management systems
Verify
Rent roll, delinquencies, property condition and zoning
Watch for
New supply under construction in the area

Pest control

Recurring residential and commercial service plans with seasonal peaks.

Profit drivers
Recurring plans, route density, technician productivity
Verify
Licences and chemical handling compliance, plan renewals, vehicle fleet
Watch for
Regulatory licences that do not transfer automatically
