Show us your business, or the one you want to buy

Four free contests run every month. Winners are chosen by judges on merit, not by chance, and get featured in front of buyers, owners, brokers and lenders. The current round closes on the last day of this month.

Business Pitch Spotlight

For owners. Pitch your business in up to 500 words: what it does, why customers stay, and where it could go next.

Prize: 90-day featured placement on the listings board, a homepage spotlight and a newsletter feature.

My First Acquisition Plan

For buyers. Describe the business you want to buy, your budget, how you will finance it and your plan for the first 100 days.

Prize: a featured interview on the site and a newsletter profile, plus sponsor prizes when available.

Best Turnaround Story

For anyone. Tell the story of a small business that was struggling and how it was turned around. Real numbers welcome.

Prize: publication as a featured article with author credit, plus sponsor prizes when available.

Creator Challenge

For creators. Make a video or article that teaches one part of buying, valuing, financing or selling a business.

Prize: a place in our video library or guides, promotion in the newsletter and a paid commission offer for a follow-up piece.

Cash and gift-card prizes are added when a sponsor funds them. Each month's full prize list is posted on this page before the round opens and never reduced during a round. Sponsor a prize.

Official rules

No purchase or payment is necessary to enter or win. A purchase or payment does not improve your chances.

1. Organiser

The contests are organised by BusinessForYou.com ("we"). Contact us through the contact form.

2. Eligibility

Open to individuals aged 18 or older (or the age of majority where they live, if higher) in jurisdictions where the contest is lawful. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Our team members, judges, sponsors' employees and their immediate families are not eligible.

3. Entry period

Each round runs for one calendar month and closes at 11:59 p.m. U.S. Eastern Time on the last day of the month. Entries received after the close are considered for the next round.

4. How to enter

Submit the online entry form on this page. Limit one entry per person per contest per round. Entries must be your own original work, in English, and must not contain confidential information you are not allowed to share, defamatory or unlawful content, or material that infringes anyone's rights.

5. Judging

These are contests of skill. Entries are scored using the published criteria and weights. Judges' decisions are final on all matters relating to judging.

6. Prizes

Prizes for each round are those listed on this page when the round opens. Prizes are non-transferable and have no cash alternative unless stated. We may substitute a prize of equal or greater value if a listed prize becomes unavailable. Winners are responsible for any taxes on prizes.

7. Winner notification

Winners are notified by email within 14 days of the round closing and must respond within 7 days, or an alternate winner may be chosen. Winners' first names, cities and entries may be published on the site with their consent.

8. Licence to publish

By entering, you grant us a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to publish, edit for length and promote your entry on BusinessForYou.com and its channels. You keep ownership of your work.

9. Privacy

Entry details are used to run the contest and contact you, as described in our privacy policy.

10. General

We may cancel, suspend or modify a round if fraud, technical failure or any other factor impairs its integrity, and may disqualify entries that break these rules. To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for lost, late or incomplete entries. A list of winners is available on request through the contact form.
