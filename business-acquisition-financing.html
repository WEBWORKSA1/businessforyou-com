Reading the result

Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) is the cash flow left after a market salary, divided by a year of loan payments. Many acquisition lenders want to see at least about 1.25x, meaning $1.25 of cash flow for every $1 of debt payments. Below that, one slow quarter can mean missed payments.

Cash-on-cash return shows what your own money earns once the business pays you a normal salary and covers its debt. Payback is how many years of that surplus it takes to recover your cash at closing.

What acquisition lenders check

  • Three years of business tax returns and year-to-date statements that agree with each other.
  • Your personal credit, liquidity and how much of your own cash goes into the deal.
  • Industry or management experience that makes you credible as the new operator.
  • A purchase agreement or letter of intent, a business valuation and a transition plan.
  • Coverage after a market salary, which is exactly what the calculator above tests.
