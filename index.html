    Questions buyers and owners ask

    Is BusinessForYou.com free to use?

    Yes. Browsing, calculators, guides, buyer matching and seller valuation requests are free. The site is funded by advertising, sponsorships and referral fees paid by partners, never by charging buyers to search.

    How much cash do I need to buy a business?

    With bank or SBA financing, buyers commonly put down about 10% to 30% of the price, plus closing costs and working capital. A seller note can cut the cash you need. The loan calculator shows your number.

    How are small businesses valued?

    Most owner-operated businesses are priced as a multiple of seller's discretionary earnings (SDE): profit plus the owner's pay, perks and one-off costs. In Q3 2026 BizBuySell reported average cash-flow multiples of 2.30x for businesses selling under $1 million and 3.73x above $1 million.

    Will my business name be published if I request a valuation?

    No. Valuation requests are confidential. Listings are published without the business name unless the owner asks for it to be shown.

    Are you a business broker?

    No. BusinessForYou.com is an independent information and introduction service. We do not represent buyers or sellers in transactions, hold deposits or give legal, tax or investment advice. When you ask, we introduce you to licensed professionals.

    How do I list my business for sale?

    Use the listing form. Listing is free while we grow. We review every submission and publish verified listings with your approval.
