Matching questions

Who sees my details?

Only the BusinessForYou.com team. We share your details with a specific broker, buyer or lender only after you approve that introduction by email.

Do I have to use a partner you suggest?

No. Introductions are options, not obligations. Many people use our matches simply to benchmark the advisers they already have.

I am outside the United States. Can you still help?

Yes. We review requests from Canada, the UK, Australia, India, the UAE, Sri Lanka and elsewhere. Lender options are strongest in the U.S. and Canada; deal and broker matching works across markets.

What if I only want to talk?

Use the contact form or choose any tab above and write your question in the notes field.
